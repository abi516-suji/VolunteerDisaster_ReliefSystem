# Volunteer Disaster Relief Coordination System

## Smart Volunteer, Task & Resource Coordination for Disaster Relief

A web-based **Volunteer Disaster Relief Coordination System** designed to improve the coordination of volunteers, relief tasks, resources, and affected areas during disasters such as floods, cyclones, and earthquakes.

---

## 1. Project Overview

During disasters, manually managing volunteers, relief tasks, and resources can cause delays and inefficient coordination.

Volunteers may be assigned without considering their skills, location, availability, and experience. Critical areas may also fail to receive timely assistance, and relief resources may be distributed inefficiently.

This project provides a centralized platform to prioritize affected areas, assign suitable volunteers, manage relief resources, track tasks, and monitor disaster relief activities efficiently.

### Example

If a flood affects an area and medical assistance is required, the system can identify the area as a high-priority location and suggest suitable nearby volunteers based on their skills, location, availability, and experience.

---

## 2. Objectives

- Centralize disaster relief coordination.
- Manage disasters and affected areas.
- Register and verify volunteers.
- Match suitable volunteers with relief tasks.
- Prioritize critical affected areas.
- Manage required and available resources.
- Track relief task progress.
- Detect possible duplicate relief requests.
- Provide emergency SOS alerts.
- Provide map-based location information.
- Send notifications and alerts.
- Track volunteer contributions and service hours.
- Provide dashboards and reports.

---

## 3. User Roles

### Admin

- Manage disaster events and affected areas.
- Manage and verify volunteers.
- Create and assign relief tasks.
- Manage relief resources.
- Monitor SOS alerts and task progress.
- View dashboards and reports.

### Volunteer

- Register and manage their profile.
- Add skills, location, availability, and experience.
- View and accept suitable relief tasks.
- Update task progress.
- Submit completion reports.
- Send SOS alerts.
- Receive notifications.
- View contribution history.

### Relief Coordinator

- Monitor affected areas and priority levels.
- Verify relief requirements.
- Coordinate volunteers, tasks, and resources.
- Monitor high-priority operations.
- Verify completed relief activities.
